Architectural Spacec back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://wanqin-liu.github.io/ahkto/ArchitecturalSpace

scope note
This class comprises identifiable spatial regions within or associated with architectural works, defined by their architectural configuration, spatial boundaries, or architectural functions. Instances of this class may be enclosed, partially enclosed, or open spaces, including rooms, halls, corridors, naves, chapels, and courtyards. An architectural space may be defined or bounded by one or more architectural elements, such as walls, columns, floors, and vaults. The spatial extent of an architectural space may overlap with the physical volume occupied by architectural elements, depending on how its boundaries are defined. Architectural spaces may contain physical objects, including furniture, sculptures, and other movable or immovable objects. Instances of this class may be associated with a building as a whole or with a particular storey. They may also extend across multiple storeys and have spatial relationships with other architectural spaces, such as adjacency and containment.

Architectural Stratigraphic Unitc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://wanqin-liu.github.io/ahkto/ArchitecturalStratigraphicUnit

scope note
This class comprises physically identifiable portions of historic buildings formed through distinguishable episodes of human construction, reconstruction, modification, or repair. Each instance represents a stratigraphically coherent physical unit, which may consist of multiple materials or construction elements, with recognizable boundaries or discontinuities that distinguish it from adjacent building fabric and provide evidence of its formation history. Instances may remain physically integrated with the surrounding building fabric while retaining a distinct stratigraphic identity. Their identification does not necessarily require a precise date or attribution to a known historical event.

scope note
This property associates an identifiable physical human-made thing or place with an information resource that refers to, describes, documents, depicts, or otherwise provides information concerning it. The property supports the association of architectural heritage and its constituent components or spatial locations with heterogeneous information resources, including textual documents, photographs, drawings, audio recordings, videos, point clouds, geometric models, and building information models. The association is independent of the information resource's medium, format, production technique, temporal coverage, or documentary purpose. Information resources may concern existing conditions, historical configurations, conservation activities, proposed designs, or hypothetical reconstructions. This property establishes a general semantic association between a physical human-made thing or place and an information resource. It does not imply that the information resource provides an accurate representation of the current physical state of the referenced subject, nor does it establish the historical validity of the information it contains. More specific relationships may be expressed through additional properties where necessary.
